# Cove Brand Guide

Locked September 24, 2026. This is the source of truth for how Cove looks, sounds, and feels.

## The founding image

A cove in the mountains: a small, sheltered inlet of serene, calm, shallow water —
protected from the mayhem and storms of the open ocean outside. Hard rock keeps the
storms out; calm water holds what matters within.

**The duality (never flatten it):**
- **Masculine protection** — the breakwater, the hard shell, dark marble cliffs. Nothing gets through uninvited.
- **Feminine nurturing** — the calm water, held and cradled. The voices you love arrive on still water.

**Brand line:** *Protected by rock. Held by water.*

## Archetype

**The trusted guardian** — not the nurturing mother, not the conquering warrior.
Think private banker, five-star concierge, bodyguard: protective, attentive, deeply
caring, expressed with total restraint. (Cancer energy: the crab's hard shell, soft
interior, lunar waters — protector of home and family.)

## Voice

- **Luxury is restraint.** Short sentences. Total confidence. No exclamation marks.
- Sell the feeling, not the mechanics. "Your phone rings only for people who matter"
  beats "AI-powered call screening with transcription."
- Grandma-simple AND premium are the same thing: fewer words, obvious value, zero noise.
- The founder's "grandma test" (if she can't use it effortlessly, it's not done) drives
  every product decision — but grandma stays out of the marketing.

## Visual system

- **Dark theme is the signature.** Deep ocean abyss (`--cove-dark-bg: #0B1016`), deep-sea
  surfaces (`#111922`), sea-mist muted text (`#9AA8B2`). Dark, cinematic, calm.
- **Light theme** stays for accessibility: cream canvas, editorial, quiet.
- **Imagery:** dark marble cliffs, deep water, the breakwater. Never stock-tech, never playful.
- **Hero image:** `public/cove-hero.webp` — the breakwater. Used on landing (full wash)
  and dashboard (subtle header wash, `.page-wash`).
- **Typography:** serif headlines (Cormorant Garamond), clean sans body (Inter). Editorial, unhurried.
- **Accents:** teal (`#487878`, brighter `#6FA3A3` in dark) and muted gold (`#B88848`). Sparingly.

## Product principles

1. **So simple a kid can do it.** Ten-second setup, no settings menus, tap-to-dial.
2. **Cove tells you what happened; you mark it handled.** A screener with a follow-up
   queue — not a CRM. No pipelines, no reminders, no scope creep without a decision.
3. **Nothing demands your attention; everything waits for it.** The product is the
   absence of interruption.
4. **Flawless reliability is the luxury.** One missed important call destroys the
   premium feeling instantly.

## What Cove is not

- Not a phone seller (conversational AI selling is a different product).
- Not voicemail. Not a CRM. Not a productivity app.
- Never markets features; markets peace.
