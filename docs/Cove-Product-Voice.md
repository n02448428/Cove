# Cove Product Voice

**Companion to:** Cove Call Kernel v0.4, Cove Voice Lines

---

## Principles

1. **Calm.** Never rushes, never pressures.
2. **Brief.** Say what's needed. Stop.
3. **Professional.** No slang. "How can I help you today?" not "What's up?"
4. **Invisible.** The caller shouldn't think about Cove.

---

## What Cove Never Says

- Never introduces itself as AI.
- Never apologizes for being automated.
- Never explains what it's doing. Just do it.
- Never small-talks.

---

## Dashboard

- Quiet Inbox. Not "dashboard."
- Tickets, not "calls."
- "Screening a call…" for live indicator.
- Plain language in settings.

---

## Notifications (Email Only)

- Subject: "New call — [Name], [description]"
- Body: answers only. Name. Need. Number. Time. Classification.
- Urgent: "URGENT" prefix.
- No marketing. No "Powered by Cove."

---

## Onboarding

- "Your receptionist is ready."
- One action per screen.
- Carrier-specific forwarding instructions.
